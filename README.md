## Abstract

We disprove Rockafellar's sum conjecture by constructing maximally monotone operators whose sum is not maximally monotone despite satisfying the interior-domain condition. We give counterexamples on $c_0$ and standard $\ell^1$, with one summand a bounded positive rank-one operator defined on the whole space. We first compute the entire monotone polar of a class of graphs and characterize their maximal monotonicity by the solvability of explicit equations in the original continuous dual. For the construction on $c_0$, we couple triangular operators through a Lipschitz curve of block sums. The positive-index coordinates of each curve value have finite support, and their common limit at the two deleted endpoints belongs to $\ell^2\setminus\ell^1$. The polar formula shows that any additional polar point would require these nonsummable coordinates in the continuous dual, proving maximality. An exact pairing identity then proves nonmaximality of the sum and a finite radial bound for the first operator. A bounded surjection transfers the construction to standard $\ell^1$, where maximality is proved in the full $\ell^1\times\ell^\infty$ pair.
