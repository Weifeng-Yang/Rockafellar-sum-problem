## Abstract

We construct counterexamples to Rockafellar's sum conjecture in which two maximally monotone operators satisfy the interior-domain condition but their sum is not maximally monotone. The examples are defined on $c_0$ and standard $\ell^1$, with one summand a bounded positive rank-one operator defined on the whole space. We establish a general construction theorem that computes the entire monotone polar of a class of graphs, gives a necessary and sufficient condition for their maximal monotonicity, and shows how a positive rank-one perturbation produces a nonmaximal sum under this condition. We apply this theorem to construct the counterexample on $c_0$ by coupling triangular operators with a Lipschitz curve. A bounded linear surjection then transfers the construction to standard $\ell^1$.
